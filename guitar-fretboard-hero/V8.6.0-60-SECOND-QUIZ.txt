Guitar Fretboard Hero V8.6.0 — 60 Second Quiz

- Quiz is now a 60-second arcade run with unlimited questions.
- Removed round/10, attempts, errors, accuracy and per-question time scoring.
- Correct answer: 100 points x current multiplier (x1 to x5).
- Correct streak raises multiplier after scoring; x5 is the cap.
- Wrong answer only resets multiplier to x1. No score or time penalty.
- Difficulty progression tied to multiplier is preserved.
- Correct hitbox green flash and floating +points are preserved.
- HUD now focuses on SCORE / STREAK / RANK / TIME LEFT.
- Final result reports score, rank and correct answers in 60 seconds.
- 28-rank ladder recalibrated for the new scoring model; VIRTUOSO starts at 14,000.
- Removed remaining internal YOU naming from current ladder row state.
- Practice, Fretboard Map and shared Fretboard Core are unchanged.
